# Methods
We compared two writing workflows on 40 manuscript sections. The primary outcome was the number
of unverifiable references per 1,000 words, with bootstrap confidence intervals.

# Results
The assisted workflow produced 3.1 unverifiable references per 1,000 words (95% CI 2.4–3.8)
versus 5.7 (95% CI 4.9–6.5) for the control, a difference of 2.6 (95% CI 1.5–3.7).

# Discussion
The reduction is modest and may not generalise beyond this sample. A limitation is that only
one annotator checked each reference; the confidence interval includes effects near zero.
