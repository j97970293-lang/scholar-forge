## 4. Selection process
Two independent screeners (or two independent AI passes with human sign-off).
Disagreements go to a third reviewer. No silent defaults. Kappa and PABAK reported.
Seed studies and near-miss rechecks used as QC probes.

## 5. Data extraction
Fields: study id, citation, design, setting, N, population, exposure, comparator,
outcome definition, effect estimate, uncertainty, confounders adjusted, risk-of-bias tool,
funding, conflicts of interest, notes.

## 6. Risk of bias / quality
Tool chosen by design (RoB 2, ROBINS-I, QUADAS-2, AMSTAR 2, GRADE). Domain-level judgements
with a quoted justification — no numeric score without criteria.

## 7. Synthesis
Effect measure, model (fixed/random and why), heterogeneity (τ², I², prediction interval),
publication bias (funnel/Egger when ≥10 studies), subgroup/meta-regression (pre-specified only),
sensitivity analyses, narrative synthesis when pooling is inappropriate.
